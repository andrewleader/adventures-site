## Climb notes

Pitch one and pitch two were both about 29 m. Pitch two used exclusively 0.75 or smaller - I wish I had more 0.5. I used a 0.5 and 0.75 at the P1 anchor. Save a 0.3 for the end of P2.

Pitch four used all of my gear from 0.5 and higher. One more #1 and #2 would have been useful - it's a longer pitch, close to 40 m, and the anchor is strangely way off to the left. Tons of corner crack on this pitch and the #4 was critical, could have probably even used a 2nd #3. First half of the corner crack was pretty nice #1 size though.

Pitch five was 31 m.

Pitch six was 35 m to a belay off of a block.

## Descent notes

- R1 was 20m, traverse far climbers right, ignore the rap anchor straight down
- R2 was 30m straight down (no reason to traverse right). Got our rope stuck.
- R3 was straight down again, 29m, goes to a non-route rappel anchor
- R4 was 28m straight down to bolted chains on a clean vertical face
- R5 was 28m down and then to the right (ignore the anchor climbers left towards the corner gulley)
- R6 was down and right 21m
- R7 was 22m straight down to the ground
- Then we did two additional bolted rappels straight down from the start of the climb in order to bypass the steep ascent trail. The first rappel is short, like 20m max, but then the second rappel goes a full 30m ending on the bottom edge of the snowfield where it's less steep.
