The Lake Louise shuttle is one of the hottest tickets in Banff National Park, and it sells out fast. If you didn't manage to book weeks in advance, don't panic — a batch of last-minute seats is released **two days before each shuttle date**, and with the right prep you can grab one.

## When Last-Minute Seats Are Released

Last-minute bookings open **2 days before** the shuttle date, **at 8:00 AM MDT**. All time slots for that day are released at once, at the same moment.

For example, if you want to ride the shuttle on **Saturday** (any time slot — 7am, 9am, whatever), the entire day's inventory drops at **8:00 AM MDT on Thursday**. It doesn't trickle out slot by slot — it's a single, simultaneous release, which is why the first few minutes are so competitive.

## How to Get Your Booking

Here's the play-by-play for release morning:

1. **At 7:50am**, [sign in to the booking site](https://reservation.pc.gc.ca/create-booking/results?transactionLocationId=-2147483647&resourceLocationId=-2147483642&mapId=-2147483634&searchTabGroupId=3&bookingCategoryId=9&view=list&isReserving=true). Being logged in before the rush avoids any last-second login friction. Create an account before this if you don't have one yet.
2. **Set up your search** for your desired Arrival Date and Party Size, and click Search.

   ![Search criteria with Arrival Date and Party Size](https://leaderimages.blob.core.windows.net/images/large/xopKX7MkB-6b.jpg)

3. **Click into the time slot you want**, and then **select the Lake Louise (Last Minute) option** so you're already on the reserve page. You want to be one click away from booking, not still browsing a list.

   ![Clicking time slot](https://leaderimages.blob.core.windows.net/images/large/Mr7bqdJX1J16.jpg)
   ![Selecting the last minute Lake Louise shuttle](https://leaderimages.blob.core.windows.net/images/large/U6gkZgk4ZC5K.jpg)

4. You should see a **Reserve** button. Feel free to click it early to test — it'll say the slot hasn't been released yet. Just click OK and wait; this doesn't cost you anything.

   ![Click the Reserve button at 8:00 AM MDT](https://leaderimages.blob.core.windows.net/images/large/kr1MtLSz6mEL.jpg)

5. **When your clock strikes 8:00 AM MDT, click Reserve.** No need to refresh the page or re-run your search — just click Reserve on the page you already had open.
6. **If you clicked too early**, close the popup/error and click Reserve again.
7. **If it says all spots are taken**, you were too slow this round — see the tips below for other options.
8. **Otherwise, move fast through the checkboxes** (terms, waivers, etc.) — the slot is *not* held until those are checked. The payment step itself is more forgiving, since your cart is typically held for about 15 minutes once you're through the checkboxes.

## If You Don't Get Your Ideal Time Slot

A few strategies can meaningfully improve your odds:

- **Have backup times ready.** Open multiple tabs to later time slots. For example, if you want 7 - 8 AM, have another tab open with 8 - 9 AM selected as a second choice. When you don't get the 7 AM slot, switch to your other tab and click Reserve.
- **Keep clicking search** if everything looks taken. Spots often reappear at random intervals — commonly 5–15 minutes after 8:00 AM — as other users' holds expire or people back out of checkout.
- **Know the cutoff.** By around **8:30 AM**, released seats have mostly been claimed and hold-expiry reappearances slow way down. If you haven't secured a seat by then, it's usually time to try again on a different day.

## Well Before Release Day

A little prep goes a long way:

1. **[Create an account](https://reservation.pc.gc.ca/create-booking/results?transactionLocationId=-2147483647&resourceLocationId=-2147483642&mapId=-2147483634&searchTabGroupId=3&bookingCategoryId=9&view=list&isReserving=true)** on the booking site ahead of time. Don't leave account creation for the morning of — you don't want to be filling out forms while seats are disappearing.
2. **Familiarize yourself with the site.** Know where the date picker, party size selector, and time slot list are.
3. **Do a practice run.** Try booking at 8:00 AM PDT a day earlier than what you need and walk through adding a slot to your cart. You don't need to pay — just get comfortable with the flow so there are no surprises on release day.

## Quick Reference

| Step | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Account creation & practice booking | Any time before release day |
| Sign in | 7:50 AM MDT |
| Slot search + reserve page loaded | Before 8:00 AM MDT |
| Click Reserve | Exactly 8:00 AM MDT |
| Recheck for reappeared spots | 8:05–8:30 AM MDT |
| Odds drop significantly | After 8:30 AM MDT |
